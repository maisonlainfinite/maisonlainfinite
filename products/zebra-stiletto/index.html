Details & composition

A classic pump balanced on a razor-sharp stiletto heel, with embossed leather panels and a sleek pointed toe. Butter-soft Italian leather wraps the shoe and heel. The Maison’s logo is showcased at the side of the heel counter.

Complimentary express shipping to Europe, the United Kingdom and the USA. Shipping to South Africa is $60. Other destinations receive an individual quote before an order is confirmed.
