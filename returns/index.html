Our approach

If your creation is not right, please contact maisonlainfinite@outlook.com with your order reference, the item concerned and a description of the issue. We assess requests fairly in light of the product, order terms, condition and reason for return. Assessment helps establish the appropriate remedy; it does not remove a refund, repair, replacement or cancellation right provided by applicable law.

The Zebra Stiletto satisfaction guarantee

The Zebra Stiletto is offered with a 100% satisfaction guarantee: love your shoes or send them back for free. Contact Client Services to arrange your return and receive the appropriate instructions and prepaid return method. We will review the item and your request in line with that guarantee and any applicable statutory rights. Please do not arrange paid return shipping without contacting us first where a prepaid method is available.

L’Ivoire and other creations

For Sac L’Ivoire, requests are assessed individually, including whether the item is faulty, incorrect, not as described, or eligible for a change-of-mind return under the order terms or applicable law. Any discretionary return arrangement and its shipping terms must be confirmed before purchase. We do not apply an undisclosed blanket “no refunds” rule.

Making a request

  1. Email the Maison with your order reference and request. Photographs can help us assess damage or a fault, but do not send payment-card details.
  2. We will confirm the relevant return instructions, address and any documentation required for international transit.
  3. Pack the creation carefully. Where reasonably possible, include the original packaging, accessories and labels.
  4. Once received, the Maison will inspect the return and explain the outcome, remedy and any lawful deduction, with reasons.

For a change-of-mind return, please keep the item in saleable condition and try footwear only on a clean, soft indoor surface. A lack of original packaging does not automatically remove statutory rights for faulty or misdescribed goods.

Cancellations and individually made goods

Tell us promptly if you wish to cancel. A genuinely personalised item or one made to your individual specifications may be treated differently under applicable cancellation law. Manufacturing an item after ordering does not, by itself, establish that every legal cancellation exception applies. Any relevant restriction must be explained before you place the order. Rights concerning faulty, unsafe or misdescribed goods remain unaffected.

Refunds

Where a refund is approved or legally due, it will be returned through the original payment method where practicable, unless another lawful arrangement is agreed with you. We will confirm the amount and processing arrangements. Any mandatory refund deadline takes priority. Financial-institution processing times may apply after a refund is issued.

Delivery and return costs will be handled according to the applicable guarantee, agreed terms and law. We will not make an undisclosed restocking deduction. Any permitted deduction for diminished value must be justified and communicated.

Legal protections

Mandatory rights in the jurisdiction applicable to your purchase prevail over this policy. Where applicable, South African consumer protections and European or UK distance-selling and product-conformity rights remain available. Contact us if you need help exercising them; an internal authorisation process must not obstruct a valid legal claim.
