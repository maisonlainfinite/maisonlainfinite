Cookies and similar storage

Cookies are small records a website can save in your browser. Local storage is another browser feature used to retain information between visits. This policy covers both. Essential storage supports features you request, such as your shopping bag and privacy preferences. Optional analytics remain off until you choose to enable them.

What this website stores

NamePurposeDuration
li_consentEssential cookie recording your privacy choice and policy version.180 days

These entries do not contain passwords, payment-card details or the information typed into contact, newsletter or membership forms. Local shopping bag data is not a secure account or order record. In this edition, optional browsing records stay on your device: no external analytics, advertising or cross-site tracking provider receives them.

Manage or withdraw consent

Choose “Essential only” to decline optional storage, “Accept optional” to enable it, or “Manage preferences” for a specific choice. Both acceptance and refusal leave essential shopping features available. Closing preferences without saving does not grant consent.

You can change your choice at any time using the footer. Turning analytics off removes the optional identifier and local browsing records. Expired records are discarded when the website is next used. Your browser can also block or clear cookies and storage; doing so may empty your shopping bag or make the website ask for your privacy choice again.

Third parties and future features

The hosting platform and the email service you use may operate their own necessary storage under their privacy terms. Future account, newsletter, analytics or payment integrations may use additional storage. Those features must be documented and, where required, gated behind your consent before activation. There are no advertising cookies in this edition.
