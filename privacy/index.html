Privacy notice

A clear account of how information is handled in this edition of the website.

Shipping & deliveryReturns & refundsTerms & conditionsPrivacy noticeCookie policyAccessibility

1. Who is responsible

LA INFINITÉ (Pty) Ltd operates the LA INFINITÉ brand and determines how information received by the Maison is used. For privacy questions, access requests or withdrawal of consent, contact maisonlainfinite@outlook.com with “Privacy request” in the subject line. Do not include sensitive identity or financial documents in an initial message.

2. Information you choose to provide

Contact forms, order requests, newsletter requests and membership invitation requests prepare an email for your review. The website does not silently send those forms. Until you send the email through your email service, the form is not delivered to the Maison. If you send it, we receive the information included in your message, such as your name, email address, enquiry, product selections, preferred size and chosen destination.

Online accounts and payments are not yet active. The website does not collect account passwords or payment-card details. It does not contain an operational Firebase customer database or PayPal checkout. Any future service that changes the handling of information will be described before it is introduced.

3. Device storage and technical information

Your shopping bag, selected delivery destination and privacy choices are stored on your device to support the features you use. If you opt into analytics, a random visit identifier and limited page-path records are stored locally. They are not currently transmitted to an external analytics service. Do not store private information in website URLs.

The hosting service may process ordinary connection information, such as IP address, browser details and access logs, to deliver and secure the website. An owner-only preview may also require the hosting platform’s own sign-in. That platform’s separate account and hosting privacy terms apply to its processing. See our Cookie policy for the website’s named storage items.

4. Why we use information

  • To respond to an enquiry or membership invitation request that you send.
  • To discuss and, where agreed, fulfil an order and provide aftercare.
  • To meet legal, accounting and dispute-resolution obligations where applicable.
  • To send Maison news only where we have a valid marketing permission or other lawful basis that applies.
  • To protect the website and prevent misuse in a proportionate way.

Depending on the activity and applicable law, the basis may be steps at your request before a contract, performance of an accepted contract, legal obligations, appropriate legitimate interests or your freely given consent. Optional storage and voluntary marketing requests use separate choices.

5. Marketing choices

Our newsletter form asks for a separate, unticked permission. In this edition it prepares a subscription request by email; it does not itself confirm enrolment. The Maison will process the request before sending marketing. You may withdraw marketing consent at any time by emailing us. Withdrawing does not affect necessary order messages or the lawfulness of earlier processing.

6. Sharing and international processing

We do not sell information received through this website. Information you send may be handled by email and hosting providers and, when an order is actually accepted, by the makers, payment providers, delivery partners or advisers needed to fulfil it or meet legal obligations. Access should be limited to what is necessary for the purpose.

Email, hosting and international fulfilment may involve processing in more than one country. Where applicable law requires safeguards for an international transfer, those safeguards must be in place before the relevant transfer. Additional provider and transaction details will be supplied when the relevant service becomes active. We do not claim that every future integration is already operating.

7. Retention and security

We retain received correspondence only as long as reasonably necessary for the enquiry, an accepted order, legal recordkeeping or a justified dispute, after which it should be deleted or appropriately de-identified. Exact periods depend on the record and applicable obligation. Local storage periods are set out in the Cookie policy, and you can remove device data through your browser.

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are required for information entrusted to the Maison. No internet transmission is entirely risk-free. Please use the dedicated enquiry address and avoid sending passwords or full payment details by email.

8. Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability, or withdrawal of consent. We may need proportionate verification before disclosing personal information. We will explain any lawful limitation and respond within applicable time limits. You may also complain to a competent data protection authority, including South Africa’s Information Regulator where relevant.

We do not use this edition of the website to make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects. Future loyalty tiers will be based on verified transaction records and should be reviewable by Client Services.

9. Children and updates

A Children collection does not mean the website invites children to provide personal information. Purchases and personal-information requests should be handled by an adult with legal capacity. If a child’s information has been provided unintentionally, contact us so that we can address it appropriately.

This notice will be updated when features or practices change. Please review the date above. Contact the Maison about privacy.
