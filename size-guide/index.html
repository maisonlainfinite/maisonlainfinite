Choosing your size

If you usually wear a half size, have a wide or narrow fit, or are between two sizes, please contact the Maison before placing an order. Share the size you wear in a well-fitting pointed pump and any fit preferences. Do not assume that a standard conversion guarantees the same fit across brands.

Your selection stays consistent

Switching the size display between EU, US and UK does not change the underlying preferred size in your bag. Your request records both the chosen display and its indicative EU equivalent so that the Maison can confirm it with you.
